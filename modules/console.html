✨ AI Studio — topic in, videos out

Type a topic, pick the days, tap Generate. The AI writes each day's script automatically — no copy-paste, no validation steps. Scripts land on the Dashboard as they're created.

How it works

Each script the AI writes is auto-checked: hook ≤ 15 words • scenes 3.5–6.0s • total 40–62s • 100–135 narration words • generic stock-footage phrases • #Shorts title. If the AI breaks a rule the day is marked error with the reason — just tap Generate again for that day.
